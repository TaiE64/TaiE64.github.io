  • CasCVS-Net: A Staged Multi-Task Cascade for Critical View of Safety Assessment
    Bock-Zien Toh†, Yuanchuan Ren†, Tay Aw Yu, Ng Khee Ong, Zhehua Mao, Sophia Bano
    MICCAI 2026 SafeSurg Workshop · Oral
    arXiv PDF

    CasCVS-Net assesses the Critical View of Safety in laparoscopic cholecystectomy by chaining three tasks: predicted boxes guide anatomy segmentation, and predicted masks supply the region features for CVS classification, so no ground-truth annotations are needed at inference. Staged training, from detection up to the full cascade, improves grounding of small and rare hepatocystic structures.

  • Calf-Integrated Arms for Bimanual Quadruped Loco-Manipulation
    Yan Pan, Yuanchuan Ren, Chipui Chan, Jingcheng Sun, Chengxu Zhou
    CLAWAR 2026 · Oral
    arXiv PDF

    An arm is built into each front calf of a Unitree Go2, so the robot can grasp at ground level and manipulate with both hands while all four feet stay planted. A vision-language model sequences skills from a fixed library for long-horizon tasks, shown in simulation on a cabinet task, a two-handed lift and an inter-arm handover.

  • RGBSQGrasp: Inferring Local Superquadric Primitives from Single RGB Image for Graspability-Aware Bin Picking
    Yifeng Xu, Fan Zhu, Ye Li, Sebastian Ren, Xiaonan Huang, Yuhao Chen
    IROS 2025 RGMC Workshop · Best Workshop Paper Award
    arXiv PDF

    RGBSQGrasp picks objects from cluttered bins with a single RGB camera and no depth sensor: a foundation depth model and a superquadric-fitting network recover a compact shape primitive for each object, which then guides grasp-pose sampling. Real-robot experiments show reliable grasps on unseen objects in packed bins.

† Equal contribution.
