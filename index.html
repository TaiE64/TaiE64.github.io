Key Projects

Machine Translation of Old Assyrian Cuneiform

Jan 2026

Multi-stage neural machine translation pipeline for 4,000-year-old Akkadian cuneiform transliterations into English. Fine-tuned ByT5-large (character-level seq2seq) on 1,500 parallel pairs, using byte-level tokenisation to handle rare diacritics without lossy normalisation. Designed linguistically-informed chunking at Akkadian clause boundaries for long texts, tuned beam search with Optuna, and implemented vectorised post-processing. Kaggle Silver Medal (18th).

Vision-Based Non-Contact Anemometer on Embedded Hardware

Nov 2025

Estimates wind speed by optically tracking the deflection of a gravity-restored drag flap. Real-time OpenCV pipeline on Raspberry Pi 5 (HSV segmentation, centroid extraction at 10 Hz), 3rd-degree polynomial calibration and a 1D Kalman filter. Wind-tunnel validated over 0–10 m/s: RMSE 0.461 m/s, R² 0.975.

Multi-Modal AI for Mushroom Species & Toxicity Identification

Nov 2025

YOLOv8n detection plus Vision Transformer fine-grained classification across 16 species, with regularisation against ViT overfitting. Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B via 4-bit QLoRA on synthetic data distilled from a Qwen2.5-VL-32B teacher, and grounded responses with a ChromaDB-based RAG system.

Tree-Climbing Robot with Obstacle Avoidance

Jun 2024

Co-designed and built a tree-climbing robot from concept to working prototype. Arduino Uno control with ultrasonic branch detection, custom grippers and a lead-screw linear lift; branch-avoidance logic and current sensing for motor protection. Climbs, avoids obstacles, deploys sensors and operates safely under varying loads.
